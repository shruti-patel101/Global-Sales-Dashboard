# Global-Sales-Dashboard
Global sales analysis using Power BI
About the Project

This dashboard shows the overall sales and profit performance of a business across different regions, cities, countries, and product categories.

Key Insights

Total sales are 9.60M, and total profit is 1.10M.

The business has around 26K total orders.

The overall profit percentage is 11.48%.

Technology has the highest sales, followed by Furniture and Office Supplies.

Western Europe contributes the highest share of regional sales.

Tools Used

Power BI, DAX, Data Visualization

Conclusion

This dashboard helps understand sales trends, identify top-performing product categories, and compare business performance across different regions.
